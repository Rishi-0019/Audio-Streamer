About Pav Bhaji

Pav Bhaji is a spicy mashed vegetable curry served with buttered bread rolls. It’s a popular Mumbai street food that’s now famous all across India.

Origin

  • Mumbai, Maharashtra
  • Mid–19th century
  • It was first made by textile mill workers in Mumbai. They needed a quick, affordable, and filling meal, so food vendors served a thick vegetable mash (bhaji) with bread rolls (pav).
